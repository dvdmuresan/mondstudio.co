Creatives:
Muresan David, Lazar Csenge, Horesnyi Mate

Intermezzo Festival was a one-day music event hosted at Magyar Zene Háza (the House of Music) in Budapest, celebrating the intersection of sound, architecture, and community. The visual identity aimed to capture the transient, rhythmic nature of the event — a single day filled with layered performances, improvisation, and moments of pause between sound and silence.

Art Direction: David Muresan
Motion Design: David Muresan
Graphic Designer: Csenge Lazar
3D animation: Horesnyi Mate
