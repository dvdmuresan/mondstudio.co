Project

Intermezzo Festival was a one-day music event hosted at Magyar Zene Háza (the House of Music) in Budapest, celebrating the intersection of sound, architecture, and community.

Approach

The visual identity aimed to capture the transient, rhythmic nature of the event — a single day filled with layered performances, improvisation, and moments of pause between sound and silence.
