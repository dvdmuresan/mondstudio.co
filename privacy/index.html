Information We Process

The website may process:

  • Technical information generated when the website is accessed.
  • Website usage and interaction data through Google Analytics.
  • Analytics consent preferences stored locally in your browser.
  • Information you voluntarily send when contacting MOND:STUDIO by email or through an external contact or booking service.

Google Analytics

MOND:STUDIO uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and performance.

Google Analytics loads when the website opens. Before analytics consent is granted, analytics storage is denied. In this state, Google Analytics does not read or write first-party analytics cookies, but it may send cookieless consent and measurement signals to Google.

If you choose ACCEPT, analytics storage is granted and Google Analytics may use first-party analytics cookies, including cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, for subsequent measurement.

Advertising-related consent remains disabled on this website.

Purpose and Legal Basis

Analytics: The purpose is to understand website usage and improve website content and performance. The legal basis for analytics cookies and storage is your consent.

Consent preference: The website stores mond_cookie_consent locally in your browser to remember whether you accepted or declined analytics. This preference is necessary to remember and respect your choice.

Contact correspondence: If you contact MOND:STUDIO voluntarily, information supplied in the communication may be processed in order to respond to your request.

Cookies and Local Storage

Before analytics consent, analytics_storage is denied and first-party Google Analytics cookies are not enabled.

After ACCEPT, Google Analytics may create first-party analytics cookies such as _ga and _ga_*.

After DECLINE, analytics storage remains denied and the website attempts to remove existing first-party Google Analytics cookies where technically possible.

The local-storage preference mond_cookie_consent can contain accepted or declined. It is used only to remember your analytics-consent choice and is not advertising storage.

Changing Your Choice

You can change your analytics choice at any time by selecting COOKIE SETTINGS in the website footer.

If analytics consent is withdrawn, analytics storage is set to denied and the website attempts to remove existing first-party Google Analytics cookies where technically possible.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that occurred lawfully before consent was withdrawn.

Google Analytics and Third Parties

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Technical and measurement information may be transmitted to Google when the service operates. You can learn more in Google Privacy & Terms.

Data Retention

Personal data and analytics information are retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to applicable legal requirements and the retention settings configured within the relevant services.

Google Analytics retention periods are managed through the website's Google Analytics configuration and may be updated from time to time.

Contact correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request and manage any resulting professional or legal relationship.

Your Rights

Where applicable under data-protection law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Request correction or deletion.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection supervisory authority.

In Romania, the supervisory authority is the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP).
