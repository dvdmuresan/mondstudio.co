Project

Maier Jewelry was founded in 2021 by sisters Laura and Iulia Maier, driven by a shared desire to turn freedom and creativity into a tangible practice. The brand began with stainless steel and semi-precious stone jewelry and gradually evolved toward handmade pieces crafted from precious metals. As the practice matured and Iulia became the main maker behind each piece, the brand needed a visual identity that could reflect this evolution with greater clarity and consistency.

Approach

MOND:STUDIO approached the project as a rebranding process focused on translating Maier Jewelry’s values of freedom, balance and authenticity into a more refined visual system. The new identity was developed to feel distinctive without becoming overly decorative, allowing the jewelry and its material qualities to remain central. Across visual identity, art direction and packaging, the system was designed to support the brand’s transition toward a more mature, crafted and coherent expression.
