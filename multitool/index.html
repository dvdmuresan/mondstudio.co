Project

Multitool is a concept product designed for technicians working in stage production for festivals, clubs and live events. The tool brings together a set of functions commonly used during the assembly and adjustment of lighting and stage systems, reducing the need to carry multiple separate tools while working on site. Conceived as a compact object for fast-paced production environments, it is designed to remain accessible throughout the setup process.

Approach

The concept is built around modularity and adaptability. Different tool elements can be exchanged to create a configuration that better fits the needs of individual technicians, while the compact body keeps the most frequently used functions within immediate reach. A magnetic attachment system also allows Multitool to connect to a phone or other compatible surface, helping keep it close at hand during installation work. The design combines industrial functionality with a modular product system intended to adapt to different workflows and specialist requirements.
