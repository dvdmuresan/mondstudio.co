Project

Megamontaj is a construction company based in Târgu Mureș, Romania. The project brings rebranding and web development into a single visual system, creating continuity between the company’s physical presence and its digital communication. The identity connects the scale and structure of industrial architecture with a clear, recognisable brand.

Approach

The design is built around an M-based symbol, a bold wordmark and a palette of red, black, white and grey. Architectural photography and technical drawings connect the identity to the company’s field, while a consistent graphic language carries across signage, business cards, packaging tape and presentation materials. The website extends this system through clear typography, structured layouts and a direct presentation of the company’s work.
