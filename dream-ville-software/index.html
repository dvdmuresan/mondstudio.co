Project

Dream Ville Software is a Cluj-Napoca-based technology startup working in software development and AI. As the company was establishing its presence and positioning, MOND:STUDIO was brought in to create a visual identity and creative direction that could give the brand a clear, confident and contemporary expression. The project focused on building a coherent foundation that would support the startup across its digital presence, communication and future growth.

Approach

The identity was developed around a minimalist, clean and bold visual language. Rather than relying on complex technological imagery, the system uses clarity, strong composition and restrained graphic elements to communicate confidence and precision. The visual identity and art direction were designed to feel adaptable and distinctive while remaining functional across different digital and branded applications, giving Dream Ville Software a consistent framework for communicating as the company evolves.
